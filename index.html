      How memory works

      Everything you do is remembered, but only by the people who saw it or were told about it later. People talk, so word gets around.

      What people remember about you changes what they say, what they do, and which choices you get.

      Text that memory changed is followed by a coloured tag, such as (Mira: a liar). Hover over it or tap it to see which of your choices caused it. After each choice, a note such as (↑ kind · Mira saw) shows which way it pushed you and who saw. You can turn tags off in this menu.

      Who's who lists everyone you've met and how they see you now. A dot means someone's view of you has changed since you last looked. Some people keep what they think to themselves.

      At an ending you'll see how you'll be remembered. Begin again · keep memory plays the story again, with everyone remembering your last run. Clear memory starts fresh.
