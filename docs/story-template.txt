// yourself; the player never sees them. See docs/writing-stories.md.
title: My Story
synopsis: One line that makes someone want to play it.

== characters
ana: Ana
  Your neighbour. Always in a hurry.
ben: Ben
  Ana's brother. Notices more than he says.

== traits
kind: help
selfish: take, ignore +0.5

== macros
anaLikesYou: ana sees you as more kind than selfish # Ana remembers


=== opening
present: ana, ben

{ana} has dropped her shopping all over the pavement. {ben} is watching from across the road.

* Help her pick it up
  ~ remember help, ana "helped Ana pick up her shopping"
  -> corner
* Pocket an orange that rolled your way
  ~ remember take, ana (seen by ben) "pocketed one of Ana's oranges"
  -> corner


=== corner
present: ana, ben
~ gossip ben to ana

At the corner, Ben catches up with Ana.
{ben knows take: He nods back toward you and says something you can't hear.}

* Wave to them
  -> friends if anaLikesYou
  -> strangers
* Walk on
  ~ remember ignore, ana (seen by ana) "walked past without a word"
  -> strangers


=== friends (ending)
present: ana

Ana waves back and calls you over.
{recall ana among kind, selfish: 'You're the one who {summary},' she says.}


=== strangers (ending)
They turn the corner without you.
{recall ana among kind, selfish: Ana will remember you as the one who {summary}.}
