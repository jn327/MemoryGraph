=== the_quay (ending)
present: hale, ines

Nobody is arrested. The brooch is not found. The passengers go ashore in ones and twos, glad of ground that stays still. Behind you Hale rings the ship's bell once, for the last crossing, and stands on the empty deck with her son for a long time. By the time the sun is properly up the ferry is just a boat again, and tomorrow it won't even be that.
