=== ledger

=== khipu
present: rosa

Your grandmother, Mama Rosa, lives above a hardware shop with three hundred houseplants and a radio that only gets one station. When you say the name Qhapaq Pampa she goes to the back room and returns with a bundle wrapped in a cloth that smells of cedar.

She unrolls khipu across the table: knotted cords in brown and red and a dark green that's nearly black. She reads them with her fingers. A settlement by a river. A promise of shared water. A family line that walked out of an imperial census rather than be counted. 'Ours,' she says. 'We carried these out so we would not be written down by people who wanted to own us. Do not carry them into another census.'

=== matching_cords
present: tupaq, kusi
~ remember world: khipu, tupaq, rosa, kin, shared_history

Tupaq doesn't touch the cords. He sends Kusi to the back of the house and she returns with a bundle of her own: brown and red and green so dark it's nearly black. Kusi lays them alongside your grandmother's. The knots match for forty cords and then diverge, as though two families had been telling the same story until one of them had to run.

'Your grandmother's grandmother,' Tupaq says, 'or someone who loved her.' He is quiet for a while. 'Thank you for not giving them to me.'
